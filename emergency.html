When something cannot wait

Emergency dental support, day or night.

Tooth pain, swelling, trauma, or a broken tooth deserves a clear next step. Call the office and share as much detail as you can.

Start with a call

Tell us what happened and what you are feeling.

The office will help determine whether you should be seen right away, schedule the soonest appropriate appointment, or seek another level of care. Common urgent concerns include:

Severe tooth pain

Persistent, worsening, or sudden pain that makes it difficult to eat, sleep, or function normally.

Swelling or infection

Swelling around a tooth, the gums, jaw, or face, especially when accompanied by increasing discomfort.

Broken tooth or restoration

A fractured tooth, lost filling, loose crown, or other damage that is painful or affecting function.

Dental trauma

An injury involving a tooth, the mouth, or jaw, including a loose or knocked-out permanent tooth.

Is this life-threatening?

For trouble breathing or swallowing, uncontrolled bleeding, serious facial injury, or another life-threatening emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.

Urgent care answers

When you are unsure, call.

Dental emergencies can be difficult to judge from home. A phone conversation is the best starting point for practice-specific guidance.

Can I request emergency care through the online form?

Please call instead. The online form is not monitored as an emergency service and does not create a confirmed appointment.

Do you help after regular office hours?

The practice offers extended support for urgent dental concerns after regular hours and on weekends. Call 770.461.3921 for current guidance.

Should I go to the emergency room?

Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department for a life-threatening emergency, trouble breathing or swallowing, uncontrolled bleeding, or a serious facial injury. For other dental concerns, call the practice first for guidance.

Can a new patient call with a dental emergency?

Yes. Call and describe the situation. The team can determine what help may be available and the most appropriate next step.
