Meet Dr. Ricaud

An uncommon path to truly artful dentistry.

Before becoming a dentist, Dr. Ricaud spent more than 15 years as a nationally certified dental ceramist. That foundation still shapes the way he thinks about precision, function, and natural-looking results.

A Louisiana native, Dr. Ricaud earned a B.S. in Biology from Southeastern Louisiana University and his Doctor of Dental Surgery from LSU School of Dentistry. He moved to Georgia in 2005 and has continued to expand his expertise across restorative, cosmetic, implant, and endodontic dentistry.

Helpful answers

A few things patients ask.

Still unsure? Call the office and the team will help point you in the right direction.

Do you offer emergency dental care?

Yes. The practice offers extended support for tooth pain and dental emergencies after regular hours and on weekends. Call 770.461.3921 for guidance.

Is there an option for patients without dental insurance?

Yes. The dental membership program is not insurance, but it is designed to help individuals and families without coverage save on routine visits and treatment.

What services are available in the office?

Services include preventive care, digital X-rays, oral cancer screening, periodontal care, restorative and cosmetic dentistry, implants, veneers, root canal therapy, and orthodontic treatment.

How does faith fit into the patient experience?

Our Christian faith shapes the practice’s commitment to compassion, integrity, and service. Patients of every background are welcome, and any conversation about faith or prayer is always optional and guided by each patient’s comfort.

Where is the practice located?
