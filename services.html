Ask about treatment
01

Preventive & family dentistry

Regular care helps us catch small changes early, protect healthy teeth, and give every member of the family a clear path forward.

  • Comprehensive exams
  • Professional cleanings
  • Digital X-rays
  • Oral cancer screenings
  • Fluoride and sealants
  • Custom mouthguards
02

Restorative dentistry

When a tooth is damaged or missing, treatment is planned to restore strength, comfort, and a natural appearance without overlooking the details.

  • Tooth-colored fillings
  • Dental crowns
  • Fixed bridges
  • Full and partial dentures
  • Bite evaluation
  • Complex smile rehabilitation
03

Cosmetic dentistry

Dr. Ricaud's background as a certified dental ceramist brings an uncommon understanding of shape, proportion, color, and how a smile belongs to the face.

  • Porcelain veneers
  • Smile design
  • Cosmetic bonding
  • Natural-looking restorations
  • Color and proportion planning
  • Personalized consultations
04

Dental implants

Implant placement and restoration are considered together, helping create a stable result that supports comfortable function and a confident smile.

  • Implant consultations
  • Implant placement
  • Single-tooth replacement
  • Implant-supported restorations
  • Restoration maintenance
  • Long-term planning
05

Root canal therapy

Focused treatment can relieve pain, address infection, and preserve a natural tooth. We explain each step so you know what to expect before and after care.

  • Pain evaluation
  • Root canal treatment
  • Emergency diagnosis
  • Protective restorations
  • Aftercare guidance
  • Follow-up evaluation
06

Periodontal & orthodontic care

Healthy gums and a stable bite are essential to long-term dental health. Treatment is personalized around your needs and the condition of your smile.

  • Periodontal evaluation
  • Deep cleaning therapy
  • Gum-health maintenance
  • Orthodontic evaluation
  • Alignment options
  • Retention and follow-up
