Before you arrive

Helpful answers for a confident first visit.

If your question is not covered here, call the office. We would rather help you feel prepared than leave you guessing.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Bring a photo ID, current insurance information if applicable, a list of medications, and any recent dental records or X-rays available to you. The office can confirm what is needed for your specific appointment.

Do you accept dental insurance?

Insurance participation and benefits can vary by plan. Call 770.461.3921 with your current plan information so the team can help you understand the next step before your visit.

What if I do not have dental insurance?

Ask about the dental membership program for individuals and families without coverage. It is not insurance, but it is designed to provide savings on routine visits and treatment.

Can I request an appointment online?

Yes. The online form sends a request to the office, and a team member will follow up to arrange the right time. It does not confirm an appointment immediately.

What if I feel anxious about dental care?

Please tell us. Questions are welcome, explanations are given in plain language, and the team will take time to understand what helps you feel more comfortable.

Can I see the office before my visit?

Yes. The Our Practice page includes real photographs of the exterior, lobby, waiting room, and meaningful details throughout the practice.
